How to perform Umrah

Based on the Nusuk Umrah Journey process

Step 1

Preparation and intention

Begin with a sincere intention (niyyah) to perform Umrah, enter the state of Ihram from the designated Miqat, and recite the Talbiyah.

Step 2

Arrival in Makkah

Arrive in Makkah and proceed to Masjid al-Haram, the sacred place where the Umrah rites begin.

Step 3

Tawaf

Stand near the Black Stone and complete seven counter-clockwise circuits around the Kaabah, beginning from the Black Stone.

Step 4

Sa'i

Walk between Safa and Marwah seven times. Men may jog lightly between the green markers, while women walk normally.

Step 5

Hair cutting or shaving

Men shave or trim their hair, while women cut a small portion of their hair. This marks the end of the Umrah rites and the exit from Ihram.

Step 6

Completion

With the rites complete, you leave Ihram and continue with worship, remembrance, and reflection in Makkah.

How to perform Hajj

A clear preparation guide for your sacred Hajj journey

1. Intention and preparation

Begin with sincere intention, a valid passport, proper health checks, and a complete understanding of your travel itinerary.

2. Arrival in Makkah

Perform Tawaf and refresh your heart with prayer, dua, and remembrance while preparing for the next sacred stations.

3. Arafat and Mina

Spend time in sincere supplication, follow the proper Hajj rites, and remain patient and focused on worship.
