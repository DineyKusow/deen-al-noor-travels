How to perform Umrah

Simple step-by-step guidance for your Umrah journey

  1. Make intention (Niyyah): Before entering the state of Ihram, make a sincere intention to perform Umrah.
  2. Enter Ihram: Put on the Ihram garments, make the Talbiyah, and remain in a state of purity.
  3. Go to Masjid al-Haram: Enter the Holy Mosque and make your first intention at the Miqat or upon arrival if you are already in Makkah.
  4. Tawaf: Circle the Kaabah seven times in a counter-clockwise direction, beginning from the Black Stone.
  5. Sa'i: Walk between Safa and Marwah seven times, remembering Allah and supplicating sincerely.
  6. Halq or Taqsir: Shave or trim the hair as a sign of completion of the Umrah rites.
  7. Pray and reflect: Spend time in prayer, remembrance, and dua in the Holy Masjid.

For a complete and safe experience, always follow the guidance of your travel team and local authorities.
